2. Settings: Stability ~35–40%, Similarity ~80%, Style ~40–50%, Speaker boost on.
3. Paste **Part 1**, generate, download the MP3. Then do the same for **Part 2**.
4. Save them as `video/public/vo-full/part1.mp3` and `video/public/vo-full/part2.mp3` and tell me. I'll split them into lines and re-render.

Keep the `<break time="1.5s" />` tags: those pauses are how I find where each line ends. If one line comes out flat, regenerate that part (it's cheap).
